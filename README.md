\# 🌱 EcoTrack AI



EcoTrack AI is an environmental impact tracking application built with Python and Streamlit.



It allows users to record daily activities such as transportation, electricity usage, water consumption, food, and plastic waste, then calculates their estimated environmental impact in kg CO₂e.



\## 🚀 Features



\- 🌱 Environmental impact calculator

\- 🚗 Transportation impact tracking

\- ⚡ Electricity usage tracking

\- 🚿 Water usage tracking

\- 🍽️ Food impact tracking

\- 🗑️ Plastic waste tracking

\- 💾 SQLite database for activity history

\- 📊 Environmental impact dashboard

\- 🤖 Machine learning prediction of future impact

\- 💡 EcoAI assistant for personalized suggestions



\## 🛠️ Technologies Used



\- Python

\- Streamlit

\- Pandas

\- SQLite

\- Scikit-learn
